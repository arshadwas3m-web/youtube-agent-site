Before submitting to Google OAuth verification:
- Replace the app name if needed.
- Confirm the contact email is correct.
- Add your final live URLs in Google Cloud Console:
  Homepage: https://your-domain-or-subdomain/
  Privacy Policy: https://your-domain-or-subdomain/privacy-policy.html
  Terms: https://your-domain-or-subdomain/terms.html

Important:
A free GitHub Pages subdomain may work for hosting, but Google OAuth authorized domain verification is usually easier with a real domain that you control.
