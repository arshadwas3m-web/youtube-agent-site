Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how YouTube Automation Agent handles information when used to generate YouTube video metadata and upload videos to an authorized YouTube channel.

Information we access

When the user connects their Google/YouTube account, the app may request permission to access YouTube account features needed to upload videos, manage video metadata, and complete user-requested YouTube publishing actions.

How we use Google user data

Google user data is used only to provide the app's YouTube automation features, including uploading videos, setting titles, descriptions, tags, categories, and privacy status when requested by the user.

Data sharing

We do not sell Google user data. We do not share Google user data with third parties except as necessary to operate the requested YouTube API functionality or as required by law.

Data storage

The app may store OAuth tokens or operational logs needed to keep the integration working. Access to these records is limited to the app owner/operator. Users may request deletion of stored data by contacting us at arshad.was3m@gmail.com.

Data retention and deletion

Data is retained only as long as needed to operate the service or comply with legal requirements. Users can request deletion by emailing arshad.was3m@gmail.com.

Revoking Google access

Users can revoke the app's access at any time from their Google Account permissions page: https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Security

We use reasonable technical and administrative safeguards to protect access tokens and app data. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to reduce risk.

Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact: arshad.was3m@gmail.com.
